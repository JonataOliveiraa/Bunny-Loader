# Bunny Loader 0.5.9-beta — 2026-11-22

The tenth beta. Big mods open faster and use less memory, and removing a mod no longer breaks your worlds or your touch controls.

## For players

- **Faster start with big mods:** the black screen between the logo and the title is about half as long. With Thorium alone it went from 6.4 s to 3.2 s (title at 17 s instead of 20 s); with Thorium, Consolaria and Split together, from about 11–12 s to 7 s (title at 22.5 s instead of 26–30 s). The game also uses about 600 MB less memory with those three mods. The first start after updating the app is a little slower, because the loader learns again which pictures your mods need.
- **Removing a mod keeps your worlds:** a world whose chests hold items with a prefix from a mod you removed now opens, instead of failing to load. Chests placed by a removed mod, and everything inside them, now survive saving the world without that mod and come back when you turn the mod on again. Before, one save deleted them.
- **Removing a touch-size mod keeps your controls:** a control enlarged by a mod you no longer have now uses the nearest size the loader knows, instead of leaving the world without buttons, stuck on "Drawing map 100%" or with a black or pink screen. Sizes that come from a mod you still have stay exactly as that mod set them.

## Fixes

- A broken touch sizes, touch buttons, boss bar or screen effects file no longer shows an error box over the menu: the game keeps its own settings and writes a warning to the log.
- A touch control saved with a size name the game does not know at all now gets a normal size instead of disappearing.

## Performance

- Each mod picture is loaded once, even when the game and the mod ask for it by different names, and all mod pictures (items, projectiles, NPCs, tiles, walls, buffs, dust, boss heads) are decoded in the background instead of on the game's thread.
- Mod pictures start loading during the logo and are sent to the screen while it shows, and the pictures used at the last start are fetched first.
- Registering thousands of items, tiles or NPCs no longer slows down as the list grows.

## Installing and updating

- The package name is `com.bunnyloader.beta`. It updates the 0.5.8-beta in place, with no uninstall needed.
- Terraria must be installed.
