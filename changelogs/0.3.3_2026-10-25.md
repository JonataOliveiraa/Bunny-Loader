# Bunny Loader 0.3.3 — 2026-10-25

Two new languages, Vietnamese and Thai, and English as the app's fallback language.

## For players

- **Tiếng Việt and ไทย:** the launcher and the Mod Menu are now in Vietnamese and Thai too. Pick them in **Settings → Appearance → Language**, or leave it on Automatic to follow the phone's language.
- **English is the fallback:** a phone in a language the app does not have yet (Japanese, for example) now opens the launcher and the Mod Menu in English instead of Portuguese. Portuguese is still chosen automatically on a phone in Portuguese.
- **Fonts:** the pixel font has no Thai letters and lacks most Vietnamese ones (ă, đ, ơ, ư and the tone marks). Thai letters use the phone's font, as Chinese already did. In Vietnamese the whole launcher and Mod Menu use the phone's font, so words are not split between two fonts.

## For modders

- A mod's `localizedNames` and `localizedSummaries` in `manifest.json`, and its `description_<culture>.md` and `changelog_<culture>.md` pages, now also accept `vi-VN` and `th-TH`. Terraria itself has no Vietnamese or Thai, so these reach only the launcher.

## For translators

- The app's texts are now one file per language in `app/src/main/assets/localization/` (`en-US.json`, `pt-BR.json`, `ru-RU.json`, `es-ES.json`, `zh-Hans.json`, `vi-VN.json`, `th-TH.json`). Each key is an English name that says what the text is (`PackageCountOther`, `ThemeEffectsDescription`...). A missing translation shows the English text. See `docs/nucleo/localizacao-menu.md` to add a language.

## Installing and updating

- Same signature as 0.3.2, 0.3.1, 0.3.0 and 0.2.0: installs over them, no uninstall needed.
