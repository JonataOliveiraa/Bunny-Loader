# Bunny Loader 0.5.1-beta — 2026-11-06

A beta with a new icon, a much bigger Example Mod and a fix for mod music slowing down the game.

## For players

- **New app icon.**
- **Longer NPC chats:** when an NPC has more buttons than fit, they split into pages of two, with a page button that only shows when there are two or more pages.
- **Bigger Example Mod:** it now has the Minion Boss with its own boss bar style, a worm, merchants, a town pet, critters, new food and healing items, a map layer, a touch button, a hold button and an aim joystick.

## Fixes

- **Mod music no longer slows down the game:** switching a mod's Music Box on and off quickly caused stutters of up to about 120 ms. Mod music now talks to Android on its own thread. While toggling a Music Box on a Galaxy A15, the slowest 1% of frames went from about 45 ms to about 19 ms. The game's own Music Boxes were not affected.
- **Installing the bundled mods is safer:** a failed or interrupted copy now keeps the installed version, and the mod's page shows the progress and the error. Before, a failed copy could make a mod disappear from the list.
- In the Christmas theme, disabled packages no longer share the green of enabled ones.

## For modders

- More of tModLoader's API and Example Mod are in. Start from the bundled Example Mod and tModLoader's documentation.
- `ModPlayer.SetStaticDefaults` now runs. It never did before.

## Known issues

- The new Example Mod content has not been tested in multiplayer.
- A mod that ships only `.cs` files compiles on the phone the first time it loads, and again when its files change.

## Installing and updating

- The package name is `com.bunnyloader.beta`. It installs next to the published app, with no uninstall needed.
- Terraria must be installed, as in the published app.
- The APK is about 235 MB.
