# Bunny Loader 0.5.11-beta — 2026-11-22

The twelfth beta. A small update: animated items in the Mod Menu now move the way they do in the game.

## For players

- **Animated item icons in the Mod Menu:** icons in the item list now follow the game's own animation. Items that swing back and forth in the game do the same in the menu, and items the game draws still no longer flicker through their frames. Very slow animations stay on their first frame.

## Installing and updating

- The package name is `com.bunnyloader.beta`. It updates the 0.5.10-beta in place, with no uninstall needed.
- Terraria must be installed.
