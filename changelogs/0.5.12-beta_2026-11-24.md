# Bunny Loader 0.5.12-beta — 2026-11-24

The thirteenth beta. Worlds open faster with big mods, loading tips from mods show up, shops show special currencies, and mods can now use screen shaders compiled for tModLoader.

## For players

- **Faster world entry:** the loading screen no longer stalls while it picks a tip (with the Example Mod it spent about 0.3 s and 41 MB on each tip), and entering a world with thousands of mod items skips work the game was repeating for every mod item (one step went from 189 ms to 60 ms).
- **Mod loading tips:** the tips on the loading screen now include the ones mods add, and touch players no longer get tips about the keyboard or a gamepad.
- **Special currencies in shops:** the savings row in any NPC shop now shows the shop's special currency, such as Defender Medals or a mod's own currency, not only at the Tavernkeep.
- **Bestiary filters from mods:** when mods add many biome filters, the filter list can be scrolled by dragging.
- **New launcher look:** a new title and new settings, folder, star, search and switch icons.

## Fixes

- Immunity to a mod buff no longer stays on after it should end, NPCs that a mod makes immune to a mod buff are now immune, and a mod buff renewed on an NPC in multiplayer now reaches the server.
- Mod data larger than 32 KB in multiplayer (big mod configs, items with a lot of saved data) is no longer lost on the way: configs are sent in parts and other messages stay under the limit the phone can carry.
- Mods that block grappling now also block the quick grapple button, and a falling star hitting an enemy no longer counts as your own hit for mod effects.
- The front layer of a mod's surface background sits at the right height on the main menu.

## For modders

- First version of compiled HLSL shaders (`.fxc`, the files tModLoader mods ship): screen effects only, through `FxcEffect` and `FxcScreenEffect`, with tModLoader's `ScreenShaderData` parameters; tested on the emulator so far, and the API may still change. The Example Mod's Minion Boss shows one (`ExampleBossAuraEffect`). Also fixes for tModLoader issues #4829, #4066, #4494, #4924, #3484, #2552, #2934 and #779. See the wiki's Screen effects page.

## Installing and updating

- The package name is `com.bunnyloader.beta`. It updates the 0.5.11-beta in place, with no uninstall needed.
- Terraria must be installed.
