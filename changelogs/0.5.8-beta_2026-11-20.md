# Bunny Loader 0.5.8-beta — 2026-11-20

The ninth beta. You can now resize every touch control, big mods stop stuttering the first time something shows up, and the Discord status got smarter.

## For players

- **Touch control sizes:** in the controls editor (Pause > Controls > Custom > Edit, then tap a control), the **Size** slider now goes from 50% to 250% in steps of 10%, instead of the game's two or three sizes. It works for joysticks, buttons, radials and mod buttons, and the touch area grows and shrinks with the control, not only the picture. The game's own sizes are still on the slider, the percentage shows next to it, and one Undo takes back a whole drag. Sizes are kept when you leave with **Save & Exit**.
- **Discord status:** the line under Bunny Loader now names your first two mods and counts the rest ("Split, Consolaria and +4 more") instead of only "6 Mods". The status also goes away when you close the game, even when you swipe it out of the recent apps.
- **Mod list:** a package without a cover shows its own icon, enlarged and soft, instead of the category icon. The "NEW" badge is smaller, stays still, and fades away 10 minutes after you first see it.
- **Items from removed mods:** worlds and characters that hold many items of mods you no longer have keep all of them as "?" items. Before, only 64 kinds were kept.
- **Powers menu:** items that borrow another item's picture, and animated items, now show their real icon in the item list.

## Fixes

- The game no longer closes before the title on the first open after installing, when the phone or emulator is slow to start.

## Performance

- No more stutter the first time an item, a boss or a sound shows up with large mods: mod textures load in the background and are sent to the screen in small slices, and mod code is prepared while you are in the menus instead of while you play.
- Mods that look for images that do not exist no longer read the disk on every frame.
- Scrolling the mod list is smoother: pictures are kept instead of being loaded again.

## For modders

- `ModTouchButton.Animation` takes a `DrawAnimation`, like an animated item: the button, the joystick, the radial icon and the editor list play it. See the Example Animated Button in the Example Mod, which is now commented like the tModLoader one.

## Installing and updating

- The package name is `com.bunnyloader.beta`. It updates the 0.5.7-beta in place, with no uninstall needed.
- Terraria must be installed.
