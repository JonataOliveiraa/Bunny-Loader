# Bunny Loader 0.5.3-beta — 2026-11-08

The fourth beta. It has a new look for the launcher, mod accessory slots, a lighter loader for C# mods and fixes for the touch controls reported from the third beta.

## For players

- **New launcher look:** new icons, animated lists and a loading strip in place of the sunflower. The biome themes are gone.
- **New app icon.**
- **Mod accessory slots:** when a mod adds accessory slots, a new button appears in the equipment tab, left of the loadout star. It opens a panel laid out like the vanilla slots, with dye, vanity and accessory on each row and the eye to hide it. Two rows show at a time, and the ▲ ▼ buttons switch pages.
- **Mod info displays** now have the eye in the accessory tab and turn on and off like the vanilla ones. Tapping one shows its name.
- **Original Terraria check:** the app now also checks that the installed Terraria is the official one.

## New in the Example Mod

- **Three accessory slots** from tModLoader's Example Mod, including a wing slot that only works while you wear a helmet.

## Fixes

- **Mod emotes:** categories with more than 30 emotes no longer draw on top of each other. The grid scrolls sideways, and the mod emotes sit after the vanilla ones.
- **Guide:** items used only in mod recipes count as materials, and the Guide finds mod recipes, including those past the game's own recipe limit.
- **NPC chat buttons:** the Goblin's **Reforge**, a mod NPC's **Shop** and the town pet's **Pet** buttons work with a tap.
- **Interactable mod projectiles** can now be tapped like vanilla ones.
- **Radial menu of mod touch buttons:** picking a button from the radial now works.
- **Mod weapons and the aim stick:** mod items now get the right touch category, so the aim stick fires them like vanilla ones.
- **Mod items with a right-click use:** they work with the alternative fire mode of Smart Interact, and the Interact button shows their alternative icon.
- **Mod texts:** item tags such as `[i:HiveBackpack]` in NPC chat show the item, and control placeholders in tooltips show the phone's touch icons.
- **Example Mod:** the bag that can't be opened by its crafter no longer stacks with bags from other crafters, and its tooltip no longer says "Right click to open" when it can't be opened. The zombie thief no longer drops a huge stack of the item it stole.

## Performance

- **C# mods cost less per frame:** in our measurements with the Example Mod, the loader's share went down by about a quarter when idle and by about a third with many projectiles and NPCs, and it allocates about half the memory per frame. Without mods nothing changes.

## For modders

- **`ModAccessorySlot` and `AccessorySlotLoader`** from tModLoader, with saving, loadouts, dropping on death and multiplayer sync. See the bundled Example Mod and tModLoader's documentation.
- **More of tModLoader's API now exists or works.** See the [API reference](https://github.com/JonataOliveiraa/Bunny-Loader/wiki/API-Reference). `InfoDisplay.DisplayName` and the colors are there, and `Active()` is now `false` by default, as in tModLoader.
- **`AltFunctionIcon(player, alternative)`** on `ModItem` and `GlobalItem` is ours, not from tModLoader. It gives the icon for the Interact button when the item has a right-click use.

## Known issues

- **Phones:** this beta was tested on the emulator only, not yet on a physical phone. Please report any crash and what you were doing when it happened.
- **Mod accessory slots:** multiplayer sync has not been tested yet. If dragging an accessory between two mod slots drops the old one instead of swapping, please tell us.

## Installing and updating

- The package name is `com.bunnyloader.beta`. It updates the 0.5.2-beta in place and installs next to the published app, with no uninstall needed.
- Install the new APK: the new loader does not run on the 0.5.2-beta APK.
- Terraria must be installed, as in the published app.
