# Bunny Loader 0.5.6-beta — 2026-11-16

The seventh beta. Your Discord profile can now show that you are playing Bunny Loader, Explore reads the new online catalog of C# mods, and mods cost less every frame.

## For players

- **Discord status:** while you play, your Discord profile shows "Playing Bunny Loader" with the bunny icon, how many mods are on and how long you have been playing. It needs the Discord app installed and signed in on the same phone, and no Discord login inside Bunny Loader. Turn it off under **Settings > Community > Discord status**.
- **Online catalog:** Explore now lists the mods of the new C# mods repository, with the Featured carousel and the top 10 in Popular picked there.
- **Settings** group their switches into one panel per section.

## Fixes

- **Accessories** from mods applied their effects twice per frame.
- **Mod walls** could stay hidden after a mod error while drawing.
- **Projectiles** that changed type kept the old mod's data.
- **Info accessories** from mods changed the item template instead of the item you hold.

## Performance

- **Mod hooks skip vanilla things:** when no mod changes an item, NPC, projectile, tile or wall type, the game no longer stops at the mod code for it. With the Example Mod, worlds with many vanilla enemies and projectiles run lighter.
- **Drawing:** the loader's own work on the game thread dropped from 1.80 to 1.47 billion cycles over 20 seconds on our phone, with the Example Mod in a world.

## For modders

- **Logs and errors are in English**, including the hint that names a wrong parameter.
- More of tModLoader's API works; see the [API reference](https://github.com/JonataOliveiraa/Bunny-Loader/wiki/API-Reference).

## Known issues

- **Discord status** was tested on one phone only. Please report a profile that shows a "?" icon or a timer stuck at 0:00.

## Installing and updating

- The package name is `com.bunnyloader.beta`. It updates the 0.5.5-beta in place, with no uninstall needed.
- Terraria must be installed.
