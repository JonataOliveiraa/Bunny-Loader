# Bunny Loader 0.5.5-beta — 2026-11-13

The sixth beta. Mods can now add dimensions, hosting a multiplayer world is much lighter, and it fixes the Google Play copies of Terraria being refused, a kick when joining servers and the reports from the fifth beta.

## For players

- **Dimensions:** mods can add separate worlds that you travel to and come back from, with a "Loading Dimension" screen and optional transitions. The pause menu gets **Exit Dimension** while you are in one. A dimension is saved next to your world and deleted with it. In multiplayer only the host travels: every player votes first, and the others reconnect by themselves.
- **Mod names follow the game's language:** changing the language in game now also changes the names of mod items, buffs, NPCs and projectiles, and item tooltips.
- **Mod items with a right-click use** show their own icon on the Interact button and in the touch icon of their tooltip.
- **New launcher icons** for Play, Close, the Explore tab, favorites, folders, new and trash.

## New in the Example Mod

- **Three dimensions:** Example Subworld, Example Advanced Subworld and Example Arena, a dimension loaded from a world file.
- **Languages:** Portuguese is complete, and Russian and Simplified Chinese are new, as in tModLoader's Example Mod.
- **Smart cursor outline** on the Example clock, sign and sink.

## Fixes

- **Terraria bought on Google Play refused:** the official Terraria check added in 0.5.3-beta refused copies bought on Google Play on many phones. The app now only checks that Terraria is installed.
- **Kicked when joining a server:** joining a server with mods that add accessory slots, such as the Example Mod, kicked you with "invalid operation in this state".
- **Mods that depend on other mods:** turning on a library and then a mod that uses it stopped Play with a compile error. Dependencies now compile first.
- **Leaving the compile screen** no longer counts as a compiler crash. The next Play shows the compile screen again.
- **Recipes:** a mod recipe with 15 ingredients, or a game recipe broken by a mod, made the game read past the end of the ingredient list and show a "recipes" error. The broken recipe is now turned off, and the error names the recipe and the mod.
- **Pylons:** a mod tile near the destination pylon could corrupt memory when you teleported.
- **Unloaded "?" items:** holding one raised an error every frame.
- **Spiders:** with a mod that changes NPC behavior, such as the Example Mod, wall and ground spiders never switched between their two forms.
- **Mod whips** now use the whip touch controls, like vanilla whips.
- **Mod signs with text** show the game's read icon, like vanilla signs.
- **Buff tooltips** with several lines now draw in one box.
- **Dimensions:** the game's saving and loading texts no longer replace "Loading Dimension", and transitions cover the whole screen.

## Performance

- **Hosting multiplayer:** the host no longer does extra work for every item copy. On our phone, hosting with the Example Mod went from 33.9 to 21.9 ms per frame with 61 projectiles and 18 NPCs.

## For modders

- **`ModSubworld` and `SubworldLoader`** add dimensions, with `WorldFile` to load a world from your mod. They are ours, not from tModLoader: see guide 24 and the bundled Example Mod.
- **`Recipe.AddIngredient`** takes at most 14 ingredients on the phone, and `Register` refuses a recipe with an empty or overfilled ingredient list with a `RecipeException`.
- **`ItemLoader.ItemCount`** now includes the 64 unloaded "?" items. `RegisteredItemCount` counts only real items.
- More of tModLoader's API works; see the [API reference](../docs/mods-em-csharp/referencia/README.md).

## Known issues

- **Multiplayer** was tested on our emulators and one phone only. Please report anything that differs between players.

## Installing and updating

- The package name is `com.bunnyloader.beta`. It updates the 0.5.4-beta in place, with no uninstall needed.
- Install the new APK: the new loader does not run on the 0.5.4-beta APK.
- Terraria must be installed.
- The Google Play version now installs next to this APK and the BL Editor.
