# Bunny Loader 0.5.13-beta — 2026-11-24

The fourteenth beta. A new Frame Skip setting lets you choose what happens when the phone can't keep up: keep the game at full speed or let it slow down.

## For players

- **Frame Skip setting:** a new row in Settings (General), with three choices:
  - **Auto** (default): the game's own behavior. When the frame rate stays under 56 for a second, it runs up to 4 game updates per frame so the world keeps its normal speed.
  - **On:** catches up from the first slow frame, without waiting a second. At a steady 60 fps it can make movement slightly less even.
  - **Off:** one game update per frame. The game slows down instead of skipping frames, like Frame Skip Off on PC.

  The choice is kept between sessions. Pauses longer than a quarter of a second are not caught up in any mode.

## Installing and updating

- The package name is `com.bunnyloader.beta`. It updates the 0.5.12-beta in place, with no uninstall needed.
- Terraria must be installed.
