# Bunny Loader 0.5.7-beta — 2026-11-17

The eighth beta. Mods can now hook almost any method of the game, and mod NPCs that act like town NPCs can be talked to with a tap.

## For players

- **Talking to mod NPCs:** a tap now opens the chat of mod NPCs that act like town NPCs without being one, like the Example Mod's Bone Merchant. Before, the phone ignored the tap.
- **Happiness:** mod NPCs that have no happiness no longer show the happiness button in their chat.

## For modders

- **Hooks on every game type:** every type of the game now has its `On_` class, as in tModLoader: about 21,000 hooks, up from about 11,500. Types like `Minecart`, `ChestUI`, `ItemSorting`, `PortalHelper`, `LockOnHelper` and `WorldSections` can be hooked now. Your editor's autocomplete lists them all; see [Hooks](https://github.com/JonataOliveiraa/Bunny-Loader/wiki/Hooks).
- A few methods still have no hook: generic methods, and some that take or return types the loader cannot pass yet (`Rect`, `DateTime`).

## Installing and updating

- The package name is `com.bunnyloader.beta`. It updates the 0.5.6-beta in place, with no uninstall needed.
- Terraria must be installed.
