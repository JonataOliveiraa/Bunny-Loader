# Bunny Loader 0.3.0 — 2026-10-22

The first version after `0.2.0`, from Oct 19 to Oct 22, 2026. Mostly the launcher: a new look, new themes and a livelier catalog.

## For players

- **Halloween theme:** a new launcher theme with a Pumpkin Moon night sky, twisted trees, glowing jack-o'-lanterns, bats, ghosts, autumn leaves, fog and, every now and then, the Headless Horseman galloping across the screen. Some cards get a cobweb with a spider. With the theme set to Automatic, it turns on by itself from October 20 to November 2.
- **More themes:** Glowing Mushrooms (spores rising through a blue night), Desert (pyramids and blowing sand) and Hallow (rainbow trees and pixie dust). All the art comes from Terraria itself.
- **The interface follows the theme:** panels, buttons, tags and the bottom bar change color with each theme (purple and orange on Halloween, sand in the Desert, lilac and pink in the Hallow...).
- **New look:** cards, buttons and windows have the stepped pixel corners of Terraria's inventory slots, and the black drop shadow under every component is gone.
- **New bottom bar:** each tab is an inventory slot, the current one is the golden selected slot (as in the game's hotbar), and the Play button is a pixel-art disc.
- **Home:** a Discord card, catalog numbers (packages, total downloads, installed), a featured carousel and a "Popular" list ranked by downloads.
- **Explore:**
  - A **Sort** button: Most downloaded, A-Z, Smallest or Largest. The choice is remembered.
  - **New mods are highlighted:** a mod published in the last 7 days shows up at the top under "New in the catalog", with a big card and a bouncing NEW badge, and keeps the badge in the list.
  - Each package shows its download count.
- **Mod page:** shows the download count.
- **Mod updates window:**
  - It no longer disappears by itself after an update. Each row now goes from "Update" to a progress bar, then "Installing..." and "Updated", and the window only closes when you tap "Done".
  - "Update all" shows how many are left, and a failed download offers "Try again".
  - If you choose "Not now", the reminder comes back after 12 hours instead of never.
- **Settings:** a new Community section with the Discord link.

## Fixes

- After a mod update finished, its "Update" button could come back for a moment before the list was reread.
- `ModPlayer.PostUpdateEquips` now runs after the armor set bonuses, as in tModLoader, so it sees what `UpdateArmorSet` set.

## For modders

- **Hook order:** `UpdateEquips` → `UpdateArmorSet` → `PostUpdateEquips` → `PostUpdate`, the same as tModLoader (the `armor` test checks it).
- **Catalog:** published mods now record when they first entered the catalog (`publishedAt` in `index.json`); that is what makes them "new" in Explore. Mods published before this version have no date and are not marked as new.
- **Download counts** come from the GitHub Releases, added up every 6 hours into `stats.json` in the mods repository.

## Installing and updating

- Same signature as 0.2.0: installs over it, no uninstall needed.
