# Trademark policy

The code license ([LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md)) covers the code. It does not grant
any right to the project's identity. This policy covers the following marks:

- the name **Bunny Loader**;
- the bunny logo, the app icon, and the title art (`_icons/launcher/titulo.png`,
  `app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/img_title.png`, `ic_bunny_*`);
- the package name `com.bunnyloader` and the `dev.bunnyloader` namespace as the
  identity of a published app.

The point is simple: people must be able to tell the official app apart from
everything else, and a modified or paid copy must not pass as the real thing.

## Allowed without asking

- Saying your fork, mod, tool or video is **"based on Bunny Loader"**, **"for
  Bunny Loader"** or **"compatible with Bunny Loader"**, as long as nobody would
  take it for the official app.
- Using the name and the logo to talk about the project: reviews, tutorials,
  videos, wiki pages, news.
- Building the app from source for your own use without changing anything.

## Required for forks you distribute

- A **different name**. It must not start with or contain "Bunny Loader".
- A **different icon and title art**.
- A **different `applicationId`**, so your build never installs over the
  official app or receives its updates.
- A note near the top of the README or the about screen saying the fork is
  unofficial and is based on Bunny Loader, with a link to this repository.

## Not allowed

- Publishing any build, official-looking or not, under the name "Bunny Loader"
  or with the logo, except the official releases from this repository.
- Names that copy the brand with a suffix, such as "Bunny Loader Pro", "Bunny
  Loader Plus" or "Bunny Loader Premium".
- Pages, Discord servers, store listings or social media accounts that present
  themselves as the official Bunny Loader.

## Official releases

The only official builds are the ones published in this repository's
[Releases](https://github.com/JonataOliveiraa/Bunny-Loader/releases), signed with
the project's release key. The README lists the certificate fingerprint so you
can check a downloaded APK.

## Questions and reports

To ask for permission, or to report a copy that uses the name or the logo,
open an [issue](https://github.com/JonataOliveiraa/Bunny-Loader/issues).
