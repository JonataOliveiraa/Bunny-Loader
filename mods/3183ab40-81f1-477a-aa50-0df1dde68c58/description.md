# Wild and Horned

Sixteen animated Touhou companions inspired by **Wild and Horned Hermit**,
ported to Terraria Mobile through Bunny Loader. Craft a summon item, use it
or equip it in the pet slot, and bring a little of Gensokyo along with you.

Each companion follows you, sways, blinks, looks around and performs her own
actions. Nearby companions from this mod can start conversations and answer
one another. Dialogue, item names, tooltips and configuration labels are
available in **English and Brazilian Portuguese**.

## The companions

| Companion | Personality and actions |
|---|---|
| Kasen Ibaraki | Lectures, sneaks steamed buns and transforms into an oni with a cloud of smoke. |
| Yuugi Hoshiguma | Raises her sake dish for a toast and laughs. |
| Komachi Onozuka | Takes a nap and yawns while supposedly working. |
| Seiga Kaku | Opens a hole with her hairpin and laughs mischievously. |
| Byakuren Hijiri | Meditates and twirls her parasol. |
| Aunn Komano | Wags and barks; her light turns orange when hostile creatures approach. |
| Okina Matara | Opens a back door; her starlight twinkles and becomes brighter at night. |
| Kosuzu Motoori | Reads, waves and giggles; her lamplight turns violet while reading. |
| Mamizou Futatsuiwa | Adjusts her glasses, smokes her pipe and becomes a tanuki teakettle. |
| Nazrin | Dowses, swings her pendulum and looks smug; nearby treasure turns her light gold. |
| Suwako Moriya | Hops, spins her iron ring and laughs under her frog-eyed hat. |
| Kanako Yasaka | Presents her shrine, plants an onbashira pillar and laughs. |
| Inu Sakuya | Begs for treats, serves tea and shakes her ears. |
| Shion Yorigami | Begs, releases cosmetic misfortune flames and sighs. |
| Jo'on Yorigami | Fans herself, puts on sunglasses and flips a coin. |
| Momiji Inubashiri | Scouts, plays dai shogi and raises her sword on alert. |

**Light pets:** Aunn, Okina, Kosuzu, Nazrin, Suwako, Inu Sakuya and Jo'on.
They hover in front of the player; the other companions hover behind.
You can equip one ordinary pet and one light pet together.

The companions do not deal damage, cut tiles, steal money or apply bad luck.

## Kasen's transformations

Open **Mod Config** from the pause menu to change whether Blood Moons keep
Kasen in her oni form, whether her seal can slip spontaneously, the average
interval between shifts (1–60 minutes), and their duration (10–300 seconds).
Defaults: both options enabled, an eight-minute average interval and a
45-second duration.

## This port

This Bunny Loader version includes its own pet runtime and does not require
Touhou Little Friend. Every item is obtainable through its original recipe.
The original Mysterious Gap vendor belongs to Touhou Little Friend; its two
shops can receive these items if a compatible Bunny Loader port provides
`touhoupets/YukariPortal` and the `Shop` / `Shop2` shops.

The tModLoader versions of Touhou Little Friend, Pet House and Pet House
Stories cannot be loaded by Bunny Loader. Conversations with characters
outside this mod and Pet House housing are not included in this standalone
port. The original four `Mod.Call` query commands are retained for future
Bunny Loader companion mods.
