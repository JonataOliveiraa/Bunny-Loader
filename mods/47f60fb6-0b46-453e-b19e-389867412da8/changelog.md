# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- Support for Terraria's languages: English, Portuguese, Spanish, French,
  German, Italian, Polish, Russian, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional),
  Japanese and Korean.
- Fixed HeadRotation.
- Fixed the petting animals check.

## 1.0.0

- Improved Movement created: the head follows the cursor, the character
  faces the cursor and the body leans while moving (standard, alternate or
  spin), on mounts too.
- Slimes, zombies and critters lean with their movement.
- Config for every option, plus leaning strength, hiding the neck accessory
  while the head is turned, no turning on ropes and the swinging weapon
  flicker fix.
