# Improved Movement

Makes your character and the creatures around you move more naturally.

- Your **head** follows the cursor.
- Your character **faces** the cursor (always, only while using an item, or
  never).
- Your body **leans** as you move (standard, alternate with jumps, or spin),
  on mounts too.
- **Slimes** lean when they jump; **zombies** and **critters** lean when they
  walk.

## Config

Everything can be turned on, off or tuned in the mod's config: head
following, facing the cursor, leaning style and strength, mount leaning,
hiding the neck accessory while the head is turned, no turning on ropes, the
swinging weapon flicker fix, and leaning for slimes, zombies and critters.

Available in English, Portuguese, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Polish,
Russian, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Japanese and Korean.
