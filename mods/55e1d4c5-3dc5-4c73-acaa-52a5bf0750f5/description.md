# WorldGen Previewer

Watch your world take shape while it is being generated. A live map of the
world fills in as each step runs, and you can pause, step through, repeat,
skip or cancel the generation.

## The screen

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Steps** | Shows or hides the list of generation steps. |
| **Previous** | Pauses and runs the last step again. |
| **Play** | Resumes the generation. |
| **Pause** | Pauses after the current step. |
| **Next** | Runs one more step, then pauses. |
| **Structures** | Shows the protected structures (green and red). |
| **Cancel** | Stops the generation. The world is not saved. |

Tap a button to use it, or hold your finger on it to see what it does. Hold
it on the map to see the name of the block under it.

Drag the map to move it, and use **+** (next to the other buttons) to zoom. While zoomed in, the map
fills the whole screen and shows every block, like the in-game map. Use the
buttons at the bottom: **+** to zoom in, **X** to leave the zoom and get the
steps list and the controls back, and **-** to zoom out.

## The steps list

- Tap a step to keep generating and pause right after it.
- Tap the **X** next to a step to skip it (tap again to keep it).
- White: still to run. Gray: will be skipped. Red: done. Yellow: skipped.
  **(x2)** means the step ran twice.

## Config

**Start Paused** pauses after the first step, so you can skip or repeat the
early steps. **Map Preview** turns the live map on or off.

Port of *WorldGen Previewer* by jopojelly (tModLoader).

Available in English, Portuguese, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Polish,
Russian, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Japanese and Korean.
