# Changelog

## 1.1.6

- Fixed a crash when going back from the Multiplayer menu after picking a
  character.

## 1.1.5

- Only the **+** button stays next to the other buttons below the map.
- While zoomed in, three buttons at the bottom of the map: **+** to zoom in,
  **X** to leave the zoom and **-** to zoom out.

## 1.1.4

- The **+** and **-** buttons now sit next to the other buttons below the
  map, so the map no longer covers them.
- While zoomed in, the buttons are always drawn on top of the map.

## 1.1.3

- While zoomed in, two buttons at the bottom of the screen: **+** to zoom in
  more and **X** to leave the zoom in one tap.
- The map fills in from left to right as it is generated.
- Smoother map updates: only the part of the map that changed is updated.

## 1.1.2

- Faster map updates on more devices.

## 1.1.1

- Fixed the screen flickering while the world is being generated.

## 1.1.0

- New layout: the map is at the top and the controls are at the bottom.
- The game's own world generation screen and logo are hidden while the
  preview is open, so nothing overlaps the map.
- Sharper map: the overview now uses up to 2048 pixels of width, and zooming
  in shows the area on screen at full detail, one pixel per block like the
  in-game map.
- Zooming in shows the map on the whole screen and hides the steps list and
  the controls. Zoom out to bring them back.
- Bigger zoom buttons on the right side of the map, always drawn on top.
- Faster map updates.

## 1.0.0

- WorldGen Previewer for Bunny Loader: a live map of the world while it is
  being generated.
- Pause, resume, next step, repeat the previous step and cancel.
- Steps list: pause after any step, skip steps, see which steps are done,
  skipped or repeated.
- Protected structures overlay.
- Touch controls: drag to move the map, zoom buttons, hold to see the block
  name.
- Map Preview option to turn the live map off.
- Support for Terraria's languages.
