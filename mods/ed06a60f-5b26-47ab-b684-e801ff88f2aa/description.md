# Vein Miner

Break **one** ore block with your pickaxe (or drill) and every matching block
connected to it (sides and diagonals) breaks too, 10 per tick, from the
closest to the farthest.

Included by default: every **ore** in the game, **gems**, **silt**,
**slush**, **desert fossil** and **obsidian**.

## Your own blocks

Hold the block (the item that places it) and type in chat:

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/vein add` | Adds the block you are holding to the list. |
| `/vein remove` | Removes the block you are holding. |
| `/vein list` | Shows your list. |
| `/vein clear` | Clears your list. |
| `/vein on` / `/vein off` | Turns Vein Miner on or off. |

The list applies to every world and character.

## Config

**Vein Miner** (on/off), **Ores**, **Default List**, **Added Blocks** (with a
button to clear them) and **Max Blocks** per vein.

Available in English, Portuguese, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Polish,
Russian, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Japanese and Korean.
