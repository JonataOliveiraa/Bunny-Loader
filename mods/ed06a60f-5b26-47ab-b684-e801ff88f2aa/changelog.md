# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- Support for Terraria's languages: English, Portuguese, Spanish, French,
  German, Italian, Polish, Russian, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional),
  Japanese and Korean.

## 1.0.0

- Vein Miner created: break one ore with your pickaxe and the whole vein
  comes with it (ores, gems, silt, slush, desert fossil and obsidian),
  10 blocks per tick, from the closest to the farthest.
- `/vein` command to add the block you are holding to the list, remove it,
  list, clear and turn the mod on or off.
- Config: Vein Miner, Ores, Default List, Added Blocks (with a clear button)
  and Max Blocks.
- In multiplayer, every broken block is sent to the server.
