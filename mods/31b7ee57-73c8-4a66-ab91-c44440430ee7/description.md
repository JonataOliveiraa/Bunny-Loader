# Abuse Goblin

You can now hit the **Goblin Tinkerer** with any weapon to lower his reforge
prices.

- Reforging starts at **50%** of the item's value.
- Every hit (melee or projectile) lowers it by **5%**, down to **5%**.
- After **15 minutes** without a hit, the price goes back to the start.
- If the Goblin Tinkerer dies, the price resets.
- The new price pops up over his head on every hit.

The price is saved with the world.

## Config

**Unkillable** (your hits never kill him), **Show Price**, **Start Price**,
**Discount per Hit**, **Min Price** and **Reset (min)**.

Port of the tModLoader mod *Abuse Goblin*.

Available in English, Portuguese, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Polish,
Russian, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Japanese and Korean.
