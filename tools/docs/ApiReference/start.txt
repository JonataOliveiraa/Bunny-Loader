# The mod itself
Mod|The mod's main class. Every mod needs one. It holds `Load`, `Call` and `HandlePacket`.
ModSystem|Code for the whole mod: world data, recipes, interface layers, world generation.
ModPlayer|Data and hooks attached to each player.
ModConfig|Options the player can change in the game.
ModCommand|A chat command.
# Items
ModItem|An item: weapon, tool, armor, accessory, material, potion...
ModPrefix|A prefix, such as Legendary.
ModRarity|A rarity, with its own color.
# NPCs and enemies
ModNPC|An enemy, a boss or a town NPC.
ModInvasion|A wave-based event. Only in Bunny Loader.
ModBossBar|The health bar of a boss.
ModBossBarStyle|Replaces the whole boss bar display.
ModEmoteBubble|An emote.
# Projectiles and buffs
ModProjectile|A projectile: arrow, magic bolt, minion, whip, hook...
ModBuff|A buff or debuff.
# Player
ModMount|A mount or minecart.
ModHair|A hairstyle.
ModResourceOverlay|Draws over the hearts and the mana stars.
# Blocks and the world
ModTile|A block or piece of furniture.
ModWall|A wall.
ModBannerTile|An enemy banner tile.
ModPylon|A pylon tile.
ModTileEntity|Data and updates attached to a placed tile.
ModTree|A kind of tree.
ModCactus|A kind of cactus.
ModBiome|A biome.
ModBiomeConversion|A conversion, such as the one the Clentaminator does.
# Scenery and effects
ModSceneEffect|Music and scenery that apply under a condition.
ModSurfaceBackgroundStyle|A surface background.
ModUndergroundBackgroundStyle|An underground background.
ModWaterStyle|A water style.
ModWaterfallStyle|A waterfall style.
ModCloud|A cloud.
ModDust|A dust particle.
ModGore|A gore piece.
# Interface and input
ModMenu|A title menu theme.
ModMapLayer|Draws icons over the map.
ModAchievement|An achievement.
ModKeybind|An input binding, a touch button on the phone. Not derived: created with `KeybindLoader.RegisterKeybind`.
ModTouchButton|A button on the screen. Only in Bunny Loader. Not derived: created with `TouchButtonLoader.RegisterButton`.
ModTouchJoystick|A button the player drags to aim. Only in Bunny Loader. Not derived: created with `TouchButtonLoader.RegisterJoystick`.
# Change what already exists
GlobalItem|Every item, from the game or from any mod.
GlobalNPC|Every NPC.
GlobalProjectile|Every projectile.
GlobalBuff|Every buff.
GlobalTile|Every tile.
GlobalWall|Every wall.
GlobalPylon|Every pylon.
GlobalBossBar|Every boss bar.
GlobalEmoteBubble|Every emote.
GlobalBackgroundStyle|Every background.
# Also content, without Mod in the name
DamageClass|A damage class.
ExtraJump|An extra jump, like the Cloud in a Bottle.
InfoDisplay|A line of information accessories.
BuilderToggle|A builder accessory switch.
PlayerDrawLayer|A layer of the player's drawing.
