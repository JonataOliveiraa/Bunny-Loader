# Plan: Super Graphics

Status: version 1.0.0 built and tested on the MuMu (2026-11-17); not yet measured on a phone.
A client-side C# mod that improves how the world
looks: light, glow, eye adaptation, water (reflection, refraction, caustics, underwater), and
the sky, with the space layer and its stars as the headline feature.

**It is a mod like any other: it uses only the public Bunny Loader API that exists today.** No
change to the loader (C++ or the BunnyLoader DLL) is part of this plan. If something turns out
impossible with the current API, the feature is cut or simplified, and the user is asked before
any loader change is proposed. (The first version of this plan had a loader phase "F0"; it was
rejected.)

- Mod folder: `C:\Users\nadek\Downloads\SuperGraphics` (format 3: `SuperGraphics.cs` and the
  `.csproj` at the root, internal name `SuperGraphics`, display name "Super Graphics").
- Rules: no comments in the mod's code, English identifiers, one class per file, UI text in
  Localization (pt-BR, en-US), logs in English, own art only (textures generated by a script).

## 1. API the mod uses

| Need | API |
|---|---|
| Post-processing shaders | `ModScreenEffect` on `ScreenEffects.Layer.World` (between the world and the UI), `cmd.Target`, `cmd.Blit`, `cmd.DrawShader`, `ScreenGraphics.Sources.Screen`, uniform arrays (`ScreenGraphics.Array`, up to 256 floats each) |
| Water and lava positions | `TileData.TileLiquid`, `TileLookup`, `TileBHeader`, `TileSHeader` (raw pointers exposed by the proxy; read with `unsafe`, no bridge call per tile) and `Main.tile._width` |
| Light level for eye adaptation | `Lighting.GetColor(x, y)` on a sparse grid (~40 calls a frame) |
| Sky, stars, Milky Way, nebulae, aurora | `On_Main.DrawStarsInBackground` (draws in the background stage, behind every background layer), `Main.spriteBatch` with the mod's own PNGs, `BlendState.Additive` |
| Sun/moon position, sky color, atmosphere | `Main.time`, `Main.dayTime`, `Main.atmo`, `Main.ColorOfTheSkies`, `Main.cloudAlpha`, `Main.screenPosition`, `Main.worldSurface`, `Main.GraveyardVisualIntensity`, `Main.shimmerAlpha` |
| Biome and state | `Player.ZoneSnow`, `ZoneSkyHeight`, `ZoneUnderworldHeight`, `ZoneRockLayerHeight`, `Player.wet`, `lavaWet`, `honeyWet`, `shimmerWet` |
| Settings | `ModConfig` (`ConfigScope.ClientSide`), sliders and toggles |

What the API cannot do, and how the plan works around it:

- **No light map texture for shaders.** Bloom works from the screen itself (bright pixels are
  where the lights are); the light map is only sampled sparsely on the CPU for exposure.
- **No shader layer behind the backgrounds.** The sky is drawn as sprites in the star hook,
  which is exactly where vanilla stars are drawn; the shader layers only post-process.
- **No per-tile texture upload.** Water is sent as up to 12 rectangles (one per water body,
  merged from column runs), each drawn as its own box so the GPU only touches water pixels.
- **A draw box limits what the screen copy covers.** Each water box therefore includes the
  band above the surface that the reflection reads.

## 2. Constraints and budget

Measured on the user's phone (Mali-G57 MC2, 2340x1080): full frame 12–14 ms GPU without
effects; one effect pass costs 0.82 ms on a 101k px box and 1.24 ms on a 494k px box.

Budget: **High ≤ 3 ms GPU, Medium ≤ 2 ms, Low ≤ 1 ms**, 60 fps held. CPU on the C# side
≤ 0.4 ms a frame (water scan amortized over several frames, sparse light samples, ~150 star
sprites). The look stays pixel art: no blur on sprites or tiles; distortion and reflection
offsets snap to the game's pixel grid.

## 3. Frame pipeline (one `ModScreenEffect`, World layer, fixed high `Order`)

| # | Pass | Resolution | Reads | Writes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Water, lava, honey, shimmer (one draw per body, max 12) | full, box only | screen | screen |
| 2 | Bright pass (soft knee threshold, luminance-weighted) | 1/4 | screen | `bloomA` |
| 3 | Dual Kawase down | 1/8, 1/16 | targets | targets |
| 4 | Dual Kawase up | 1/8, 1/4 | targets | targets |
| 5 | Sun shafts (radial blur of the sky-bright mask toward the sun) | 1/4 | `bloomA`-level screen sample | `rays` |
| 6 | Composite: bloom + rays + exposure + filmic curve + grading + vignette + dither | full | screen, `bloomB`, `rays` | screen |

Underwater (camera in liquid) adds a full-screen wobble and tint inside pass 6.
Quality presets drop the 1/16 level, halve ray samples or turn passes off.

## 4. Features

### 4.1 Light and glow
- Bloom with a soft knee, tinted by the light color, strength by setting; stronger at night
  and underground, weaker at noon.
- Exposure / brightness, contrast, saturation, vibrance, temperature (warm–cool) and a
  shadow lift setting.
- Split toning: dark areas lean cool, lit areas lean warm (the "adaptation to shadows" look).

### 4.2 Eye adaptation
Average log luminance of `Lighting.GetColor` on a 9x5 grid around the player, every frame.
Target exposure = clamp(0.4 / avg, 0.8, 1.45), mixed with the setting; dark→bright adapts in
~0.6 s, bright→dark in ~3 s.

### 4.3 Sun and moon shafts
Sun position from the same formula as `Main.DrawSunAndMoon` (time, scene width, `bgTopY`).
Mask = bright sky pixels in the 1/4 screen sample; 16–24 radial samples (GPU Gems 3 ch. 13).
Only near the surface, during the day (moon rays at night, faint), scaled by `1 - cloudAlpha`.

### 4.4 Water
Liquids come from a scan of the visible tiles (+8 tiles margin) every 4 frames: column runs
of `liquid > 0` merged into rectangles with the surface height from the top tile's amount.
Per body:

| Effect | How |
|---|---|
| Reflection | Mirror around the surface line, offset by two scrolling noise layers, fade with depth, Fresnel-like blend; snapped to pixels |
| Refraction | Small animated offset of the color below the surface |
| Caustics | Animated abs-sine/warped cell pattern added under the surface, fading with depth, scaled by scene brightness |
| Surface | Bright glint line and soft foam at the surface; rain ripples when `Main.raining` |
| Lava | Heat haze above the surface, orange glow, slow crust flicker |
| Honey | Thick, slow distortion, amber tint |
| Shimmer | Iridescent hue shift |
| Underwater | Screen wobble, depth tint, light shafts from above |

### 4.5 Sky and space (the headline)
Drawn in `On_Main.DrawStarsInBackground` when enabled (vanilla stars skipped; falling-star
items are unaffected).

- **Real sky.** The Yale Bright Star Catalog (5th rev., public domain, 9,096 stars) is baked
  offline: the 814 stars down to magnitude 4.6 go to `Assets/Sky/BrightStars.bin` (RA, Dec,
  V magnitude, B−V) and are drawn as sprites at their true positions, with the color from B−V
  (Ballesteros → blackbody), PSF glow, and diffraction spikes below magnitude 1.5. Fainter
  catalog stars, 16k procedural magnitude 6.5–8 stars weighted toward the galactic plane and
  the Milky Way (galactic coordinates, so the band, the Great Rift and the Sagittarius bulge
  sit where they are in the real sky) go to one equirectangular `SkyMap.png` (2048x640,
  Dec +90 to −40). The view spans 120° of right ascension; the sky turns 15° per game hour.
  Nebulae and galaxies are drawn at their real coordinates (M42, Rosette, Pleiades, Veil,
  North America, Eagle, Lagoon, M31, M33). Generator scripts: `make_sky_map.py`,
  `make_sky_art.py` (numpy + Pillow).
- **Visibility** follows the vanilla rule (sky color brightness, graveyard, shimmer, clouds),
  so stars fade in at dusk and **show by day in space** (`Main.atmo` → 0 turns the sky black).
- **Twinkle scaled by atmosphere**: strong near the surface, gone in space (no air).
- **Milky Way** band with dust lanes, **nebulae** and two small **galaxies**; brighter and
  more saturated in space.
- **Shooting stars** at night, more often in space.
- **Aurora** curtains in the snow biome at night, animated.
- Slow sky drift with time and a little horizontal parallax.

### 4.6 Finish
Filmic curve that keeps highlights from clipping, biome/time grading (cool night, warm sunset,
desaturated underworld heat), vignette, interleaved gradient noise dither against banding.

## 6a. Results (MuMu 16384, beta 0.5.7, 2026-11-17)

- Builds with no warnings locally and on the device compiler (`Main.dedServ` instead of
  `netMode == Server`, which trips BL0001).
- Probe mod (scratchpad, not in the repo) drove noon, dawn, night, space by night and by day,
  a cave with torches, a lake, diving, lava and a snow biome, each with the mod on and off,
  in Low, Medium and High: no shader compile error, the effect spawns and despawns with the
  switch, 7 passes a frame on Medium.
- Average GPU time of the effect on the MuMu: 2.2–2.7 ms (the MuMu is not a phone reference).
- Fixed during the passes: the rays' sky mask counted dark pixels as sky (the whole night
  turned blue), contrast around 0.5 crushed dark scenes (now an S-curve on luminance that
  keeps black), rays in space (no air, now off), caustics too strong, aurora placement.
- Screen captures on the MuMu lag 2–4 s: hold each probe scene ≥ 10 s and shoot early, or the
  capture lands in the next scene.
- The `.bl` is built with `tools/mods/pack.py` from a copy without `bin/`, `obj/`, `tools/` and
  the `.csproj`; the art generators live in the mod's `tools/` folder.

## 6b. Version 1.1.0 (2026-11-17)

- Water bodies carry the bottom of every column (up to 128, `uniform vec4 uColumns[32]`), so
  sloped beaches and bowl-shaped pools are covered to the bottom (before, the shallowest
  column cut the body to a thin band, which the user saw on the phone).
- Reflection rebuilt after studying Luminous Water Reflection 潮之韵 (star·kz, Workshop
  3578165070, no license: ideas only, our own code): strong (0.75), linear fade over 80 game
  px, 0.9 vertical scale, 55% sharp / 45% soft samples, per-row wobble. Their mod draws
  `Main.waterTarget` into its own target and marches up the mask to find the surface per
  pixel; the phone has no such target, so we keep the per-column bodies.
- Caustics tinted by the water's own color (their "colorful wave" idea), two layers of the
  warped-sine pattern; underwater light shafts, depth tint, rising bubbles, mist above the
  surface at night and underground (their fog spawns vanilla gores; ours is in the shader).
- Sun/moon halo occluded by the sky mask; wider bloom halo from the 1/16 level; ambient light
  motes tinted by biome; deeper vignette underground.
- Sky in higher resolution: catalog stars down to magnitude 6.3 drawn as sprites sized in
  physical pixels (`XNAUnityRunner.ScreenWidth`), `SkyMap.png` 4096x1024 with the Milky Way
  only, a 1024 `StarField` tile at 1.5 physical px per texel, nebulae 512, galaxy 256,
  aurora 512x256.

## 6c. Version 1.2.0 (2026-11-17)

- Light motes moved out of the composite shader (they showed everywhere, inside solid ground)
  into CPU particles (`Common/Ambient`): spawned only on open tiles (`TileProbe`, raw tile
  pointers), drawn additively in `PostDrawTiles`, each bright one calling
  `Lighting.AddLight`. Fireflies only at night near the ground (they fade at dawn); spores,
  sparkles, embers, ice crystals and pollen by biome; dust in sunlight and in torchlight
  (`Lighting.GetColor` ≥ 0.3). Caps 20/36/64.
- Water effects scaled by the light of the pixel itself (caustics, shafts, bubbles, glint):
  nothing shows in the dark.
- Mist only on snow-biome surface water, long streams (≥ 12 tiles) at dawn and night, and a
  low cold mist over cave water (≥ 8 tiles); a slow time variation (0.3–1.0); edges fade over
  40 game px past the banks instead of cutting at the draw box; it lightens toward the mist
  color instead of adding light.

## 6d. Version 1.3.0 (2026-11-18)

- Water moving with the camera: the screen-effect uniforms lag the image by exactly one frame
  (the pending frame sent after `DoDraw` N is consumed when the world camera renders N+1).
  Measured on the MuMu with a red marker drawn by the game in `PostDrawTiles` plus its
  reflection in the water, frame by frame from `screenrecord`: offset = −0.57 × marker step
  per recorded frame (≈ one game frame), 0 when still. The CPU side is in sync
  (`screenPosition` during the tile draw = after `DoDraw`). The mod predicts the next camera
  (`SceneCamera`: position + last step, no prediction past 160 px) and shifts its masks:
  mean |offset| 4.5 px → 0.7 px. The loader itself still lags every world-anchored effect.
- The underwater pass was screen-wide (keyed by the tile at the screen center), so the sky
  above the surface got the tint and the wobble whenever the camera crossed the surface. It now
  uses the camera's water body (surface + per-column bottoms) as a mask; the wobble follows
  world rows; the underwater shafts fade in the dark.
- Scanner: liquid inside solid tiles is not water, platforms do not cut water, water under a
  ceiling has no surface. The crimson shaft of the user's picture was a lit box with sharp
  pink lines: caustics followed per-tile lighting and the max channel of red water. Light is
  now a 5-tap average (±10 game px) weighted by luminance; gain raised so blue water matches.
- Ambient particles rebuilt as habitats fixed to the world: 24×16-tile cells surveyed from the
  raw tile arrays (re-survey every 10 s); grass/jungle/hallow ground gives fireflies at night
  (jungle also underground, sparse), glowing mushroom grass gives spores; no cell with ≥ 3 evil
  tiles in the cell plus 16 tiles around, none within 240 rows of the bottom. Motion, blink and
  wake/sleep times are functions of a seed and the game clock, so they are where they were when
  the player comes back. 6×6 pixel sprite (2×2 core) with point sampling + a faint halo. Dust,
  specks, embers, sparkles, pollen and evil spores removed.
- Life crystals (tile 12): heartbeat pulse, red `AddLight`, redraw of the animated tile frame
  (`Main.tileFrame[12] * 36`) additively, soft glow and rising sparks.
- Moonlit clouds: hook on `NextHorizonRenderer.DrawCloud` (the phone draws straight to
  `Main.cloudBatch`); an additive copy (`Color` with A = 0 works there) offset toward the moon
  goes under the cloud and leaves the rim, plus a light wash near the moon. Strength by moon
  phase and altitude, tint for blood/pumpkin/frost moons.
- Space: a dark veil (`SkyFade.png`) darkens the top of the sky as `atmo` drops, and each layer
  shows below its own sky brightness (stars by magnitude 155 → 54, Milky Way 56, deep sky 60),
  instead of the vanilla visibility formula that let the Milky Way blaze over a purple dusk.
  The sun halo shrinks in space. Aurora removed.
- Probe (scratchpad, not in the repo): the user's world copied as the test world, scenes for
  the ocean, crimson shafts, forest, jungle, evil surface, mushroom, life crystal, clouds and
  10 altitudes; old and new side by side. Not measured on the phone yet.

## 6e. Version 1.3.1 (2026-11-18)

- Report: "with zoom in or out, the water shifts while flying". Tested on the MuMu with the
  zoom set by code (`GUIInstance.Active.GUIZoom.ApplyPixelZoom`, 0.7/1.0/1.5 → virtual screen
  810/1158/1736 wide), by the on-screen buttons, by the keyboard (`input keyevent` 69/70) and by a
  real pinch (multitouch `sendevent` on `/dev/input/event4`, slots 0/1), with teleported and real
  flight (velocity −7) and with frames slowed to 2–4 updates per draw. A red line drawn by the game
  3 px above the surface stayed on the shader's surface in every case (median 8–10 phys px, the
  expected gap). `Main.screenWidth` matches the world camera size (±1 px); `MagnifyActive` is the
  cursor magnifier (`GUIMagnify`), never the zoom. The moving shift was not reproduced.
- What was reproduced: the dark-blue steps above sandy slopes in the user's picture. The 1.3.0
  scanner stopped a column at the first solid tile, and half blocks and slopes (header bit 0x400,
  slope bits 12–14, checked in `Tile.halfBrick`/`Tile.slope`) hold water in their open half. Such a
  tile now ends the column at its middle (`TileProbe.Partial`/`HoldsLiquid`). Test: a pool with a
  sand ramp of half blocks and slopes built by the probe.
- Additive `SpriteBatch` on the phone ignores the color alpha: `Color(r, g, b, a)` adds the full
  rgb. The crystal redraw at "alpha 0.1" blew the heart to white; firefly fades and blinks, star
  twinkle and the gradual Milky Way did not happen (only the on/off gate). All additive tints are
  now premultiplied (`rgb * a, a`). The crystal pulse is the tile itself brightened in its own
  colors plus a short halo; the sky reveal curve is `sqrt` of the old ramp so night skies keep
  their Milky Way (weaker under a full moon).

## 6f. Version 1.3.2 (2026-11-18)

- Report: the water still jumps for an instant when starting/stopping a climb or a fall and
  when zooming, then settles. Measured with a camera step scene (48 px jumps) recorded frame by
  frame: with the 1.3.0 prediction every change costs two bad frames (one late, one overshooting
  by the step that did not happen); without prediction, one late frame.
- Pairing measured with two stamps: the probe stamps `ScreenEffects.Frame + 1` into the image
  in `PostDrawTiles`, a test composite stamps the frame id of its uniforms. Image N carries the
  effect of frame N−1 in 395 of 420 readable frames (N−2 or N in the rest). The world camera
  renders once per `DoDraw`, outside it, and every marker issued is consumed in the same frame
  (no backlog). Calling `ScreenEffects.Tick` from inside `DoDraw` did not change the pairing, so
  moving the tick in the loader is not a fix: the uniforms are bound to a render that shows the
  geometry of the following `DoDraw`.
- Fix in the mod: `FrameStamp` draws 4 cells of 3×3 game pixels at the top-left corner with
  `screenPosition.X & 511`, `screenPosition.Y & 511`, `screenWidth & 511` and a signature,
  right after `Main.DrawInfernoRings` (after the foreground water; from `PostDrawTiles` the
  water covered it when the camera was deep). Water and composite shaders read the camera of
  the image itself (uniforms only seed the wrap), the water masks get slack, the bright pass
  skips the cells and the composite hides them. If the signature fails, the prediction stays.
- The world framebuffer on the MuMu is RGB565 with dithering (red/blue 5 bits, ±1 LSB noise):
  6 bits per channel broke; 3/4/3 bits per cell with wide bands decode exactly.
- Results: camera steps with 0 bad frames, pinch zoom while flying aligned, 0 fallbacks in 657
  frames including the camera deep underwater; still scenes match 1.3.1.

## 6g. Version 1.3.3 (2026-11-18)

- Cost: the composite decodes the stamp (4 texture reads per pixel, full screen) only while the
  camera is underwater; elsewhere it uses the uniform camera, which only matters for hiding the
  stamp cells. Draw options, column uniforms (`WaterScanner.ColumnUniform`) and the underwater
  column array are reused across frames instead of rebuilt. Firefly fades live in
  `Habitat.Fades` instead of a `(cell, index)` dictionary; habitat eviction runs every 30 ticks.
  The effect reference is cached in `GraphicsSystem` instead of a lookup per update.
- Layout: `SuperGraphicsEffect` only orchestrates; `WaterPass`, `PostPass`, `Underwater` and
  `Common/Scene/SunLight` hold the rest. The stamp decoder is `Assets/Shaders/Stamp.glsl`,
  inserted after the `#include` lines by `EffectShaders.LoadWithStamp` (`Shader.FromSource`).
- Tested on the third MuMu instance (16448, beta 0.5.7, world `wldreal`, the 16384 was in use by
  another session): still scenes match 1.3.2 apart from caustic animation, camera steps aligned.
- Five TODOs (three in English, two in Portuguese) and a few extra blank lines were added on the
  user's request.

## 7. Risks

- `BlendState.Additive` and `End/Begin` inside the background stage on the phone's
  SpriteBatch (batches group by texture; restart the batch around our draws).
- Water rectangles approximate odd-shaped bodies (caves); limited to 12 bodies.
- Other mods' screen effects: Super Graphics uses a high `Order` so its composite runs last.
